2-bromobutane: R or S?

One molecule is R and the other is S. Rotate each model, rank the groups attached to C2, and assign its configuration.

1Use the dropdowns beside the groups to rank 1–3. H is 4.2Turn H away and follow 1 → 2 → 3.
How do I decide R or S?

With the lowest-priority group, H, pointing away from you, follow 1 → 2 → 3. Clockwise is R; counterclockwise is S. A flip rotates the entire model, so its configuration stays the same.

How do I rank the groups?

Start with the atom directly bonded to C2: a higher atomic number gives a higher priority. If two groups begin with the same atom, compare the atoms attached to those atoms, in decreasing atomic number, until you find a difference. Exclude the bond back to C2.
