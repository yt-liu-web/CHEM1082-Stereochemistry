Think through the flip

Axial ⇄ equatorial.
Up stays up. Down stays down.

The same carbon is highlighted in both chairs. Each carbon needs one axial and one equatorial bond, and one up and one down bond.

Up/down names the ring face. An equatorial bond can be up or down; “equatorial” does not mean “down.”

Get a drawing hint

The six axial bonds are parallel and alternate up/down around the ring. Equatorial bonds point outward, roughly along an extension of a nearby C–C bond. At the same carbon, the equatorial bond is on the opposite face from its axial bond.
