CHEM 1082 · CONFIGURATION LAB

cis & trans: 1,2-dimethylcyclopentane

Can rotating a single bond move a methyl group to the other face of a ring?

Rotation changes conformation. It preserves configuration.

Rotate a C–CH₃ bond and watch the three methyl hydrogens turn. The methyl carbon stays on the same ring face.

Read the wedge–dash structures

A solid wedge points toward you (face +). A hashed wedge points away (face −). An unlabeled line end represents CH₃ here. The structures keep a fixed view even when you turn the 3D model.

Change the configuration

To interchange these stereoisomers in this model, break C2–CH₃ and C2–H, exchange CH₃ and H, then form the new bonds. Turning an intact σ bond cannot perform this exchange.

Idealized teaching geometry: the ring is drawn flat to make its two faces clear; real cyclopentane is puckered. Colors identify groups, not elements. Break → switch → form is a conceptual stereochemistry operation, not a reaction mechanism.
